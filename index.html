A family dream, shared

Rooted in Medellín. Made for the table.

Founded in Medellín in 2008, Brasaland began as a family dream: sharing the authentic taste of grilled meat with consistent quality and warm service. Today, our 14 restaurants span two countries, while fresh ingredients, traditional techniques, and care for every dish remain at the heart of what we do.
